white-space Property

White-space: nowrap

The nowrap value prevents text from wrapping to the next line, even when it overflows the container. Consecutive white spaces are still collapsed into a single space.
It Collapses white spaces and keeps all text on a single line, preventing line breaks.

This text will not wrap even if the line is very long. It will continue on one line and overflow outside the box boundaries. Spaces still collapse, but wrapping is disabled.
